The cavity and planar-Poiseuille exercises derive from OpenCFD OpenFOAM v2512
tutorials. Retained headers identify their origin. OpenFOAM is distributed under
the GNU General Public License; see https://www.openfoam.com/legal/licensing
and the corresponding OpenCFD source distribution for license terms and notices.
The GPL v3 text is included in simulation/OPENFOAM_COPYING.txt.

The repository does not redistribute the OpenFOAM or ParaView executables or
downloaded research papers. The Docker helper references the original OpenCFD
image by immutable digest.

Reactor dimensions and gas-property assumptions are attributed in the included
geometry and physics JSON files. NIST-derived property data are attributed in
physics/nist-3d/operating-point.json. The scientific model is an independent,
simplified project implementation, not vendor equipment CAD.
