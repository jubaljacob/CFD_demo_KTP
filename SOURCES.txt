Research and software sources

Kimes, Moore and Maslar (2012): https://doi.org/10.1063/1.4742991
Reactor geometry inspiration. This demonstration uses a simplified isothermal
nitrogen operating point, not a reproduction of the paper's complete physics.

Burgess (2024), NIST Technical Note 2279:
https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.TN.2279
Gas diffusion reference.

OpenCFD OpenFOAM v2512: https://www.openfoam.com/documentation/tutorial-guide
Local CFD solver and finite-volume tutorials. OpenFOAM is third-party GPL
software; its executable/source distribution is not bundled in this static site.

ParaView: https://docs.paraview.org/en/latest/
Local post-processing and exported flow paths/tracer video. No ParaView
executable is bundled.

ChatCFD: https://doi.org/10.1002/aidi.202500174
Agent-assisted CFD design inspiration; not a reproduction of its implementation.

AI CFD research: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06607
Design discussion/reference; not an implementation or verification of that paper.
